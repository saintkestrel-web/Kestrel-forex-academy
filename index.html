Build your trading foundation

Level 01

Forex Fundamentals

Understand currency pairs, pips, lots, spread, leverage, and margin.

Preview lesson

A currency pair compares two currencies. In EUR/USD, EUR is the base currency and USD is the quote currency. Learn position size and pip value before risking money.

🕯️
Level 02

Candlesticks & Structure

Learn candle anatomy, swing highs and lows, trends, BOS, and CHoCH.

Preview lesson

Start by marking clear swing points. An uptrend generally forms higher highs and higher lows; a downtrend generally forms lower highs and lower lows. Context matters.

🎯
Level 03

ICT & Smart Money Concepts

Study liquidity, fair value gaps, order blocks, displacement, and mitigation.

Preview lesson

These are ways traders interpret price action, not guarantees of future movement. Define each concept consistently and test your rules on historical charts.

🛡️
Essential

Risk Management

Plan risk per trade, position size, Stop Loss, Take Profit, and risk-to-reward.

Preview lesson

Choose the amount you can afford to lose before entering. A Stop Loss does not guarantee the exact exit price during gaps or fast markets.

🧠
Mindset

Trading Psychology

Build patience, consistency, and a process for reviewing mistakes.

Preview lesson

Use a journal to record the setup, entry reason, risk, result, and whether you followed your rules. Review decisions, not only profits and losses.

📝
Practice

Backtesting & Journaling

Turn ideas into rules and evaluate them before risking real funds.

Preview lesson

Write objective entry and exit rules, test a meaningful sample of trades, include costs, and avoid changing the rules after every loss.

Your roadmap

Learn in the right order

Do not rush into advanced setups before you understand the basics.

STEP 02

STEP 03

Test & review

Backtest, journal, and practise on a demo account.

A rule worth keeping

Protect your capital first.

Forex trading carries substantial risk. Education can help you make informed decisions, but it cannot guarantee profits. Practise on demo before trading live.

No. Learn the basics, practise on a demo account, and understand the risks before considering live trading.

No. Every trading method can lose. Risk management and testing are essential.
